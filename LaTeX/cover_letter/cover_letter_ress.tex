\opening{Dear Editors,}

I submit for consideration the manuscript \textbf{Forecast-based removal of turbofan engines with sensor-only prognostics and simulated borescope inspection}.

The paper asks when a turbofan engine should be removed for restoration when removal is decided from a forecast of the functional-failure endpoint and from an explicit, held-out margin on that forecast, rather than from a detected onset of degradation. It uses the C-MAPSS benchmark with strict separation of fitting, calibration and evaluation engines. The protocol was frozen before the multi-condition subsets FD002 and FD004 were opened, and they were opened once. Adverse results are reported: the landmark of the health index supplies almost all of the life gained, the index is unstable under changes of the fitting engines, and some rules show endpoint events in FD004. A simulated borescope arm, whose rejection can only advance the forecast decision, is evaluated against sensor-only rules. A 20-year restoration scenario is conditional on an explicit and unvalidated scale between simulated and flight cycles.

The study fits the scope of the journal on test and maintenance policies, models for ageing and life extension, uncertainty analysis and automatic fault detection and prognosis. No maintenance credit, regulatory compliance or population-level risk is claimed.

The manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere. There is no funding to declare and no competing interest. The use of generative AI tools is declared in the manuscript. The C-MAPSS data are public; the code and derived outputs will be deposited in a public repository.

\closing{Sincerely,}
